# Cat-tree visual editorial decisions — 18 Oct 2026

Seven original AI illustrations in the small-apartment cat-tree guide depicted hanging pompoms and/or tall structures with unverified stability. The images are now **explicit caution examples**: all six in-article captions and the hero caption disclose the visible risks. The article adds a buyer risk-assessment section, pointing to Cornell's small-part and string precautions and clearly requiring manufacturer support, dimensions, load and mounting evidence.

Seven images have `status: approved` **for editorial depiction with warnings**, NOT certification that the pictured objects are safe or products were tested. Do not promote actual depicted structures as recommended SKUs without separate product testing/specification verification.

All 23 previously flagged WebPs have now received individual editorial dispositions. **Five new SVG illustrations still have `updated-pending-review` and must be rendered and visually inspected.** Production publication remains blocked in `scripts/audit-visual-assets.mjs` and `scripts/check-launch.mjs`.
