# Mew & Maple — Pre-publication cat-welfare risk triage
**Date:** 17 October 2026. **Status:** editorial screening only; NOT veterinary endorsement.

This document maps the existing eight draft articles to topics that should be excluded, qualified or referred to a qualified veterinarian/behavior professional. It does not establish that any animal, toy, product or home setup is safe for every cat. All posts remain `status: review`.

| Draft article slug | Priority risk | Claims and visuals needing targeted review | Required editorial guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `diy-cat-toys` | HIGH | Yarn/ribbon ingestion, bags with handles, torn cardboard/paper, detachable feathers, fragile homemade objects | Never market loose materials as safe for unattended use; only inspected, supervised activities; refer persistent non-food ingestion to a veterinarian |
| `entertain-cat-while-at-work` | HIGH | Unattended play equipment, window or balcony falls, overly complex food puzzles, access to litter/water | Separate genuinely independent environmental features from supervised play; closed/secured window and stable surfaces required |
| `window-perch-vs-cat-tree` | HIGH | Window mounting, suction failures, weight limits, fall risk, unstable structures | No specific weight/load guarantee without a verified manufacturer's specification; show installation as illustrative, not certified |
| `cat-trees-small-apartments` | HIGH | Tall narrow furniture, anchoring, jumping/fall injuries, reduced mobility | Explicit installation and load guidance, verify product instructions before recommending brands, no untested rankings |
| `home-for-two-indoor-cats` | MODERATE-HIGH | Intercat intimidation, access-blocking, feeding tension, inappropriate introductions or elimination changes | Multiple separated resources are a starting point rather than a promise; persistent fighting, anxiety or elimination changes need individualized professional assessment |
| `cat-friendly-apartment` | MODERATE-HIGH | Open balconies, houseplant toxicity, electric cords, retrofit furniture and wall mounts | Do not infer plant species safety from a photo; secure windows/doors; defer load-bearing installation specifics to approved instructions |
| `indoor-cat-enrichment-ideas` | MODERATE | String/wand use, food-calorie shifts, climbing, unsafe observation perches | Supervise new toys, store dangling parts after play, count treats toward diet and adapt access to cat mobility |
| `indoor-cat-play-routine` | MODERATE | Excessive play duration, older or injured cats, sudden decreased activity/limping | No fixed activity quotas; stop if distressed, panting or limping; medical symptoms need veterinary assessment |

3. **Medical referral triggers:** ingestion/poisoning concerns, new limping or reduced mobility, sudden appetite/litter-box changes, persistent aggression, distress or suspected pain. No diagnosis or treatment plan in this blog.
4. **Claim-to-source mapping:** keep citations traceable to the source paragraph; do not present an illustrative AI image as a tested installation, product photograph or proof of suitability.
5. **Reviewer policy:** a credentialed feline-health/behavior professional may be commissioned for high-risk claims, but **no such review is currently verified**. Editorial reviewers must record name, date, article and decision.
