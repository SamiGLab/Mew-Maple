## 3. Üretilen 4 Adet Instagram / Threads Kaydırmalı Gönderi (Carousel)

### Carousel 1: Kiracı Dostu 5 Kedi Kuralı
- **Kapak (Slayt 1):** *5 Apartment Cat Rules We Swear By (That Landlords Won’t Complain About)*
- **Slayt 2 (Kural 1 - Tırmalama Konumu):** Don't hide scratchers in the closet. Place sturdy vertical scratchers directly next to sofa corners where your cat already wants to mark.
- **Slayt 3 (Kural 2 - Dikey Alan):** Use freestanding bookcases with nonslip mats on top instead of wall-mounted shelves. Instant high perches with zero drill holes.
- **Slayt 4 (Kural 3 - Kum Hijyeni):** High-sided open boxes beat closed hoods. They stop scatter on small bathroom floors without trapping trapped odor that drives cats away.
- **Slayt 5 (CTA):** Want our full room-by-room layout guide? Tap the link in our bio to read the complete guide and grab our free Small-Home Cat Checklist!

### Carousel 2: 15 Dakikada Kediyi Yorma ve Geceyi Sakin Geçirme
- **Kapak (Slayt 1):** *How to Tire Out an Indoor Cat in 15 Minutes (Without Leaving Midnight Zoomies)*
- **Slayt 2 (Av Döngüsü):** Cats don't just want random waving. They need the biological sequence: Stalk → Chase → Pounce → Catch. Let them catch the lure repeatedly.
- **Slayt 3 (Yavaşlama):** In the final 3 minutes, slow down the wand lure. Imitate tired prey so your cat's heart rate lowers gradually instead of spiking.
- **Slayt 4 (Kapanış Ödülü):** End the play session immediately with a small meal or protein treat. "Hunt → Catch → Eat → Sleep" is hardwired into their biology.
- **Slayt 5 (CTA):** Bookmark this for tonight! Need a structured daily schedule? Grab our free printable 7-Day Activity Planner via the link in bio!

### Carousel 3: İşe Giderken Kediyi Yalnız Bırakma (4 Bölge Kuralı)
- **Kapak (Slayt 1):** *Leaving Your Cat Alone While at Work? Set Up These 4 Zones First.*
- **Slayt 2 (Zone 1 - Güvenli İzleme):** A secure window perch or clear sill where they can safely observe birds without loose blind cords nearby.
- **Slayt 3 (Zone 2 - Kendi Başına Avlanma):** 2 simple dry-food puzzles or toilet roll treats hidden under chairs. Never leave dangling wand toys unsupervised.
- **Slayt 4 (Zone 3 & 4 - Derin Uyku ve Su):** A quiet elevated sleeping cave away from loud appliances, plus fresh water placed far from their food bowl.
- **Slayt 5 (CTA):** What’s your cat’s favorite workday spot? Let us know in the comments and read our full guide on mewandmaple.com!
